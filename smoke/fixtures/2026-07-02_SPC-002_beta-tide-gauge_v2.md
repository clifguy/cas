# Beta Tide Gauge Survey — Certified

Certified survey for the estuary tide-gauge network. Twelve acoustic gauges
report water level at six-minute intervals to the harbor telemetry
concentrator; all twelve sites are now certified.

## Gauge Siting

Gauges are mounted on pier pilings below the lowest astronomical tide mark.
Each site records a benchmark elevation tied to the national vertical datum
by differential leveling, re-verified this season at all twelve sites.

## Salinity Correction

The acoustic travel-time correction uses the monthly averaged salinity
profile from the mid-channel conductivity sensor. Corrections are applied
at the concentrator before archival.

## Storm-Surge Annex

Surge events above the ninety-fifth percentile of the historical
distribution flag the affected interval for manual review. Flagged
intervals keep both raw and corrected series in the archive.
