# Epsilon Lighthouse Keeper Notes

Notes from the headland lighthouse rotation. The first-order Fresnel lens
turns on a mercury float bearing and completes one revolution every twenty
seconds.

## Lamp Watch

The keeper logs lamp changeovers, wick trim, and vault-room temperature at
the top of every watch. Fog signals are logged with start and end times
and the prevailing visibility estimate.

## Provisioning

The tender calls monthly with lamp oil, ledger books, and relief crew.
Provisioning shortfalls are flagged in the district quarterly return.
