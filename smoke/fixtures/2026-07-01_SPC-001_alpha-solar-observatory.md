# Alpha Solar Observatory Operations Manual

Reference manual for the ridge-top heliograph station. The station records
photospheric granulation imagery every ninety seconds during daylight and
archives calibrated frames to the basalt vault archive.

## Instrument Calibration

The primary heliograph objective is recollimated each equinox. Collimation
drift beyond four arcseconds triggers a full optical bench teardown. The
calibration ledger records lens temperature, humidity, and the technician's
initials for every adjustment session.

### Spectroheliograph Alignment

The spectroheliograph slit is aligned against the sodium D-line doublet.
Alignment is verified by comparing consecutive scans of the chromospheric
network against the reference plate library.

## Dome Maintenance

The observatory dome shutter runs on a worm-gear drive lubricated with
lithium grease at the start of each observing season. Shutter travel time
above eighty seconds indicates gear wear and schedules a drive inspection.

## Data Archival

Calibrated frames are checksummed and written to the archive in daily
bundles. Each bundle manifest lists frame count, exposure statistics, and
seeing estimates derived from the guide-camera log.
