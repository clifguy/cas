# Zeta Observatory Weather Buoy Brief

Brief on the offshore weather buoy that feeds sky-condition forecasts to
the observatory scheduling desk. Shares the observatory series code with
the operations manual it supports.

## Telemetry

The buoy reports wind, humidity, and cloud-base estimates hourly over a
packet-radio link. Missed reports beyond three consecutive hours page the
duty forecaster.

## Mooring

The mooring chain is inspected by divers each spring; link wear beyond
twenty percent schedules a replacement before the storm season.
