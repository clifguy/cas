# Theta Calibration Checklist

Season-opening checklist for the observatory instrument bench. Shares the
observatory series code so the checklist travels with the equipment it
verifies.

## Items

- Confirm heliograph objective collimation is within four arcseconds.
- Verify the spectroheliograph slit alignment against the sodium doublet.
- Grease the dome shutter worm-gear drive and time a full shutter cycle.
- Reconcile the calibration ledger against last season's closing entry.
