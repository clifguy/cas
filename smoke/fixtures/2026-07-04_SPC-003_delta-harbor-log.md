# Delta Harbor Dredging Log

Season log for the harbor approach-channel dredging campaign. The cutter
dredge works the outer bar on the ebb tide and moors inside the breakwater
overnight.

## Daily Entries

Each entry records cubic meters removed, spoil-ground coordinates, and
fuel consumed. Entries are countersigned by the harbormaster before the
weekly reconciliation.

## Channel Soundings

Post-dredge soundings run on a fifty-meter grid. Depths shallower than the
declared channel depth reopen the affected reach for a second pass.
