# Beta Tide Gauge Survey — Draft

Initial survey notes for the estuary tide-gauge network. Twelve acoustic
gauges report water level at six-minute intervals to the harbor telemetry
concentrator.

## Gauge Siting

Gauges are mounted on pier pilings below the lowest astronomical tide mark.
Each site records a benchmark elevation tied to the national vertical datum
by differential leveling.
